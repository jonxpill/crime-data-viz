THE TOLL

Western Cape · recorded murders · Apr 2008 – Mar 2026

Every dot is one recorded murder. Eighteen years of South African Police Service murder records for the Western Cape, poured out chronologically — each dot leaving the year it happened — into a single growing disc. Year one at the core; each year adds a ring; the disc never resets. What settles is the accumulated toll: 61,383 lives.

Honesty notes. Counts are real, from SAPS records; the disc's radius is the running total, so each ring's thickness is that year's loss. 2025/26 is the sum of SAPS's quarterly releases and is unaudited. These are recorded murders — what reached a police station.

Crime: SAPS Annual Crime Records 2008–2023 via DataFirst (CC-BY) + SAPS annual & quarterly releases 2023–2026 (saps.gov.za). Built on three.js with a custom GPU point-field engine — one buffer of points, every view a pure layout function. By Jonx Pillemer.
