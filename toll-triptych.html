THE TOLL

Western Cape × KwaZulu-Natal × Gauteng · recorded murders · Apr 2008 – Mar 2026

Every dot is one recorded murder. Eighteen years of South African Police Service murder records for three provinces, poured side by side into three growing discs — each dot leaving the year it happened, year one at the core, each year adding a ring that never resets. What settles is each province's accumulated toll: 61,561 lives in the Western Cape, 83,435 in KwaZulu-Natal, 79,060 in Gauteng.

All three discs pour on one calendar clock — every year takes the same time on every dial, so a heavy year arrives as a visible burst and the three provinces stay comparable year by year. Drag any dial to scrub them together.

Honesty notes. Counts are real, from SAPS records; each disc's radius is that province's running total, so each ring's thickness is that year's loss. At equal disc sizes the busier province simply burns denser — density is this piece's light. 2025/26 is the sum of SAPS's quarterly releases and is unaudited. These are recorded murders — what reached a police station.

Crime: SAPS Annual Crime Records 2008–2023 via DataFirst (CC-BY) + SAPS annual & quarterly releases 2023–2026 (saps.gov.za). Built on three.js with a custom GPU point-field engine — one buffer of points, every view a pure layout function. By Jonx Pillemer.
